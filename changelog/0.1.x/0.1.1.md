# 0.1.1 — 2026-10-01

## Added

- **`ukcrime_list_reference`** decodes force ids (British Transport Police and each force's dated coverage gaps included), crime categories, a force's neighbourhood ids, and availability: the published months and which forces published stop and search in each.
- **`ukcrime_search_crimes`** searches one month's street-level crimes in a 1-mile point radius, a polygon, a `location_id` or a neighbourhood, or a force's unplaced crimes, returning counts by category and latest outcome, the busiest map points, and a page of crimes with their `persistent_id`.
- **`ukcrime_search_outcomes`** lists the police outcomes recorded in one month in the same areas, for crimes from that month or earlier, with counts by outcome and by crime month.
- **`ukcrime_get_crime_outcomes`** fetches the full outcome history of 1–25 crimes by `persistent_id`; unknown ids land in `not_found` and failed lookups in `failed`, so one bad id never fails the batch.
- **`ukcrime_search_stops`** searches one month's stop and search in an area or a whole force, with breakdowns by type, both ethnicity fields, outcome, object of search, legislation, age range and gender; up to 8 `filters` narrow the breakdowns and the page together.
- **`ukcrime_find_neighbourhood`** finds the force and neighbourhood policing team for a point or by id: contact channels, stations, current priorities, team ranks and names, upcoming events, and optionally the boundary as a polygon the searches accept.
- **Shared inputs** — `month` defaults to the latest published month and is echoed back; `category` takes a slug or display name with case, spaces, underscores and hyphens folded ([#1](https://github.com/cyanheads/uk-police-crime-mcp-server/issues/1)); failures carry a typed `reason` and a recovery hint.
- **`PoliceApiService`** is the one path to data.police.uk: a process-wide pacer under its 15-requests-a-second limit, retries, a 50 s budget per tool call, and in-process caches. Redirects are refused, bodies are capped at 32 MB, force-written HTML reaches callers as plain text, and URL fields keep only `http`/`https` links.
- **Configuration** — no API key and no server-specific environment variables; runs over stdio or Streamable HTTP, and ships as an npm package, a `.mcpb` bundle, and a GHCR image.
- **Data terms** — every response carries the Open Government Licence v3.0 attribution and a `data_note`; locations are anonymised map points covering at least eight addresses, data spans a rolling 36 months, outcomes are police outcomes only, and each `notice` names a force's dated coverage gaps.
